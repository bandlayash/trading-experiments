## Synthesis
VLO's positives are real but slow-acting: a cheap capacity acquisition, a reaffirmed dividend, Zacks Rank #1 and strong peer margins. None of them counters the two forces driving the stock: a policy de-rating after the Democratic Senate win and a fading war premium in oil. The stock is down about 22% in a month, including an 8% single-day drop on Nov 4, with sector ETF outflows on top. Mean reversion is possible but not supported by any dated catalyst inside the window. This is a sector shock still unfolding.
